Privacy Policy

The Undesirables LLC · Last updated 30 October 2026

Support Privacy Get it on the App Store

Apple requires every app to publish a privacy policy. Most of them are long because the app collects a great deal. This one is short.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no account. There is no sign-up. There is no server of ours that receives your information. We do not know who you are, which local you belong to, who you work for, or how many hours you logged.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter:

  • your hours, dates, contractor names, pay rate and classification
  • your punch-in and punch-out times
  • the notes you dictate or type
  • your certification cards, their numbers and expiration dates
  • your paydays, chosen skin, and the name you gave the app

All of it is written to storage on your own phone. Ledger does not upload it, back it up to us, sync it, share it, or send it anywhere. We could not produce your log if we were asked for it, because we have never had it.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include Ledger's data, the same as any app's. That copy is yours, in your iCloud account or on your computer, and we never receive it.

To delete it, delete the app. From version 1.0.1, Settings → Erase everything also deletes it all except your language, skin and time zone. In version 1.0, Erase everything deletes only your work log; your cards and their numbers stay until you delete the app. Erasing doesn't reach an iPhone backup made before you erased.

Backups are yours

The app can write your work log to two files — one readable, one that restores the log. Your cards, paydays and settings are not in them. Those files go wherever you send them. Once a backup leaves the app, how it is stored and who can see it is up to you and to whatever service you put it on.

Voice notes

Speech is converted to text so you can log a day without typing. The app keeps only the text, and only on your device.

From version 1.0.1, on iPhone this is done on the phone itself; if your iPhone can't do that for your language, Ledger does not record, and you type instead. Your voice is not sent anywhere. The web version has no microphone.

Version 1.0 uses Apple's speech recognition on iPhone, which can send your voice to Apple when the phone can't do it alone. Its web version uses the browser's own, which can send your voice to Apple or Google. If your app is older than 1.0.1, update it from the App Store.

Union and pension records

The Local, Pension and Radar sections show public records filed with the U.S. Department of Labor, the State of Illinois, and the federal courts. The iPhone app carries them inside it, so reading them sends nothing about you anywhere. Those records concern labor organizations, benefit plans and employers. They name the officers and staff the filings are required to list, with their pay, but nothing in them comes from you or from what you log.

The web version

Ledger also runs in a web browser. That page is hosted by GitHub, which, like any website's host, sees your IP address and which files your browser asks for, such as which local's filings you open. Those logs are GitHub's, not ours. After your first visit the app works offline. What you log is written to your browser's own storage and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's data erases it. The browser can also clear it on its own: on iPhone, Safari may erase it if you don't open the page for about a week, unless you've added Ledger to your Home Screen. Save a backup.

Third parties

There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no third-party SDKs, and no crash reporting that transmits your content. Nothing about your use is sold or shared, because nothing about your use is collected.

If you tap a link to the Department of Labor, the Illinois Department of Labor, a court docket, or your fund's website, you leave the app and those sites' own policies apply.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be to make that clearer, not to start collecting.

Contact
