Privacy Policy

The Undesirables LLC · Last updated 4 August 2026

Support Privacy Get it on the App Store

Apple requires every app to publish a privacy policy. Most of them are long because the app collects a great deal. This one is short.

What we collect

Nothing.

There is no account. There is no sign-up. There is no server that receives your information. We do not know who you are, which local you belong to, who you work for, or how many hours you logged.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter:

  • your hours, dates, contractor names, pay rate and classification
  • your punch-in and punch-out times
  • the notes you dictate or type
  • your certification cards and their expiration dates
  • your paydays, chosen skin, and the name you gave the app

All of it is written to storage on your own phone and stays there. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, synced, shared, or transmitted anywhere. We could not produce your log if we were asked for it, because we have never had it.

Backups are yours

The app can write your log to two files — one readable, one that restores. Those files go wherever you send them. Once a backup leaves the app, how it is stored and who can see it is up to you and to whatever service you put it on.

Voice notes

Speech is converted to text so you can log a day without typing. On Apple devices this uses the operating system's own speech recognition, subject to Apple's handling of that feature. The app keeps only the text, and only on your device.

Union and pension records

The Local, Pension and Radar sections show public records filed with the U.S. Department of Labor, the State of Illinois, and the federal courts. Reading them sends nothing about you anywhere. Those records concern labor organizations, benefit plans and employers — not individual members — and contain no personal information about you.

Third parties

There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no third-party SDKs, and no crash reporting that transmits your content. Nothing about your use is sold or shared, because nothing about your use is collected.

If you tap a link to the Department of Labor, the Illinois Department of Labor, a court docket, or your fund's website, you leave the app and those sites' own policies apply.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be to make that clearer, not to start collecting.
